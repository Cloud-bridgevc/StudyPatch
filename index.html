How it works

From a ten-minute check to targeted practice

Assess

Students answer a short adaptive assessment. Questions adjust to their responses within limits you set.

Analyse

Responses map to curriculum skills. Wrong answers are matched to the reasoning they suggest, such as adding fractions across.

Draft

StudyPatch drafts practice, hints and worked examples for each gap. Every item is labelled AI-generated.

Approve & track

You edit, approve and assign. Progress reports show how demonstrated mastery changes over time.

Educational safeguards & data protection

AI that assists, and people who decide

Teacher review gate

No AI-generated question or practice reaches a student until a teacher approves it. This control can’t be switched off.

No unsupported diagnoses

StudyPatch reports evidence on specific skills. It never labels ability or suggests learning disabilities, and it says so on screen.

Clearly labelled

Every generated question, hint and example is badged “AI-generated”, with the reviewer and date shown to students.

Data minimisation

Display name and a pseudonymous ID only. No dates of birth, contact details or health information. Names never go to the AI model.

Encrypted & restricted

Built on AWS: KMS encryption at rest, TLS in transit, private networking, role-based access and row-level security.

Sensible retention

Administrators set retention windows. Nightly jobs pseudonymise or delete data on schedule, with an audit trail.

Compliance requirements (FERPA, COPPA, UK/EU GDPR and local rules) vary by customer. Design notes are in the documentation and are not legal advice.

FAQ

Questions educators ask

Do students ever see AI content that a teacher hasn’t checked?

No. Generated questions and practice start as drafts. Only content a teacher approves can be assigned, and it carries an AI-generated label.

Does StudyPatch diagnose learning difficulties?

No. It reports evidence about specific skills from specific questions. It doesn’t make claims about ability, intelligence or learning disabilities. Concerns should go through your school’s usual specialist processes.

How do adaptive assessments stay fair and predictable?

Teachers set the minimum and maximum number of questions, the difficulty range, the starting level and whether follow-up questions are allowed. Only approved questions are used, and you can preview example paths before assigning.

What student data do you collect?

A display name, a pseudonymous ID, class membership, and responses to assessments and practice. We don’t collect dates of birth, contact details or health information, and names are never sent to the AI model.

Where is data stored and for how long?

Structured data lives in encrypted PostgreSQL on AWS in your chosen region; educational resources in encrypted S3. Administrators set retention windows and nightly jobs apply them.

Can we use our own curriculum and questions?

Yes. Edit skills and objectives, write your own questions with misconception tags, or approve AI drafts into the bank.

Which subjects are supported?

The prototype demonstrates mathematics. The skill-map and misconception model is subject-agnostic and can extend to other subjects.

Is the product ready to use today?

This website accompanies an interactive prototype built with fictional data. The architecture and rollout plan describe the production path.
